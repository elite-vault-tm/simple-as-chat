Prompt 01 Start here

Who Are You Talking To?

If your content isn't connecting… this is probably why.

Help me figure out who my content is really meant for based on my [brand, niche, business, content style, or goals]. I want to better understand the type of people most likely to connect with my content, trust my message, engage with my page, and follow what I create. Include: • The type of audience this brand or content naturally attracts • Their mindset, interests, habits, and online behavior • What they are probably struggling with right now • The kind of content they would stop scrolling for • What type of wording, tone, or messaging connects with them most • What could make them trust or relate to this brand faster • Content ideas designed specifically for this audience Keep everything realistic, modern, and focused on real social media behavior.

Walk away knowing exactly who you're creating for — and how to actually reach them.

Once you know who you're really talking to… watch how differently everything starts to land. 🖤
Prompt 02

Make It Look Like You

Your content should feel recognizable the moment people see it.

Act as a strategic creative director for [brand, business, page, or project]. Help me build a complete visual direction that feels connected and recognizable. Include: • A color palette with hex codes and the feeling each color gives off • Font pairings for headings, captions, and overall brand use • The overall visual style and energy of the brand • Mood and aesthetic keywords that match the brand vibe • 3 brand or creator references whose visual style matches this direction • What would make this brand instantly recognizable online • The overall feeling people should get when they land on the page Make the final result feel intentional, modern, and easy to maintain across social media.

A complete visual direction you can actually maintain — not just a mood board that collects dust.

Okay NOW we're getting somewhere. This one changes how your whole page FEELS to people. 👀
Prompt 03 Fan favorite

Say It Better

Paste what you wrote. Get back something that actually sounds like you.

Rewrite this content: [paste content here] so it sounds more natural, conversational, and easier to connect with online. I want the final version to feel human, clear, engaging, and true to my personality — instead of sounding overly robotic, corporate, or forced. Include: • A cleaner, smoother rewrite of the original content • Stronger wording where the message feels weak or unclear • Better flow and pacing throughout • Ways to make the content easier to read on social media • Stronger hooks, transitions, or ending lines if needed • Caption-style wording that feels modern and natural • Optional CTA ideas that match the tone Keep the tone warm, conversational, confident, and easy to understand.

Content that finally sounds like a real person wrote it — because that's what people actually stop for.

Prompt 04

Plan Your Next Video

Stop staring at a blank screen. This prompt builds the whole plan.

Create a short-form video plan for [your topic] for [Reels, TikTok, Shorts, Facebook, etc]. I want this video to feel natural, attention-grabbing, and paced for social media. Include: • A strong opening hook for the first few seconds • A full talking script broken down by scene or moment • Ideas for camera angles, B-roll, or screen recordings • Suggestions for pacing and where to speed up or slow down • Caption or on-screen text ideas • Ways to keep people watching longer • A strong ending or CTA that feels natural • Thumbnail or cover text ideas Keep everything conversational and easy to film without a big production setup.

Hook, script, pacing, CTA — the whole video plan, ready before you even hit record.

Okay last one. And honestly? This might be the one people come back to the most. 👇
Prompt 05 Game changer

One Idea Should Never Just Be One Post

You do NOT need to keep starting from scratch every time you post.

Take this content: [paste content here] and turn it into multiple content pieces for different social media platforms. I want each version to feel natural for the platform it's going on — instead of sounding copied and pasted everywhere. Include: • A short-form video idea with a hook and talking points • A carousel version with slide-by-slide ideas • A Facebook post version • An Instagram caption version • A Threads or Twitter/X version • Additional short-form content ideas pulled from the main topic • CTA ideas that match each platform naturally • Suggestions for visuals or content style for each version Keep the tone modern, conversational, and easy to post across different platforms.

One idea. Multiple platforms. All done without starting over every single time.

Same idea. 6 pieces. Zero starting over.
