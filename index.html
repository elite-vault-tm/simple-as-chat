The 5 Essential Prompts

1. The Clarifier

Purpose: Get ChatGPT to ask you better questions before responding.

When to use: When your topic is complex or you're not sure what you're looking for.

Before giving me an answer, ask me 3 clarifying questions about what I'm trying to accomplish.

2. The Explainer

Purpose: Get detailed, educational explanations.

When to use: When you want to understand something deeply.

Explain this like I'm a beginner. Break it down step-by-step and use simple language.

3. The Brainstormer

Purpose: Generate creative ideas and alternatives.

When to use: When you're stuck or need fresh perspectives.

Give me 5 creative alternatives for this idea. Think outside the box.

4. The Debugger

Purpose: Fix problems systematically.

When to use: When something isn't working and you need structured help.

Help me debug this step-by-step. What could be the root cause?

5. The Optimizer

Purpose: Improve and refine existing work.

When to use: When you have something good and want to make it great.

How can I improve this? Give me specific, actionable suggestions.
Pro Tip: Combine these prompts for even better results. Stack them together in a conversation to go deeper.
